Delete your Fame Rush account

Fame Rush, published by Jeffrey Kay

Delete in the app

  1. Open Fame Rush and go to Profile.
  2. Open Settings and scroll to Delete Account.
  3. Tap Delete Account, type DELETE and tap Delete Forever.

Your account is deleted immediately and the game returns to the start screen.

Delete without the app

If you no longer have the app, email strmsupport@gmail.com with the subject "Fame Rush account deletion" and include:

  • your nickname in the game;
  • the Google email address linked to the account, if you linked one (send the request from that address if you can).

We will confirm that the account is yours and delete it within 30 days. A guest account that was never linked to Google cannot always be matched to a request, so deleting it in the app is the most reliable way. Please do not send passwords or payment details.

What is deleted

  • your account and its sign-in sessions;
  • your nickname, avatar and cosmetics;
  • your progress: Coins, XP, Fame, level, league, streak, challenges, achievements and season results;
  • your reward history and installation records;
  • friend invites and friend challenges you created;
  • the link between your Google account and Fame Rush.

Deletion cannot be undone. If you sign in with the same Google account later, Fame Rush starts a new save.

What may be kept

Deleted data can remain in our hosting provider's encrypted backups for a limited period until they are overwritten. Crash reports are kept by Firebase Crashlytics for 90 days and analytics data according to our retention settings; neither is linked to your nickname or email. Advertising providers keep their own records under their policies. Friend challenges created by other players stay with them, without your account. See the Privacy Policy for details.
