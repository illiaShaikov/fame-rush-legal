Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

This policy applies to Fame Rush, a mobile game for Android, published by Jeffrey Kay ("Fame Rush", "we", "our" or "us"). For privacy questions and requests, contact strmsupport@gmail.com.

1. Overview

Fame Rush is a free arcade game: you play quick activities to earn in-game Coins, XP and Fame, collect cosmetics and climb monthly season leaderboards. Coins, Fame and other rewards are virtual items with no cash value; they cannot be bought with, won as, or exchanged for real money or prizes. The game does not sell anything inside the app.

Your progress is saved on our game server so that it is not lost and can be restored. The game is funded by advertising.

We do not sell your personal information. Other players see only your nickname and game profile. Your Google account name and email address are never shown to other players, and you can delete your account and progress at any time in Settings → Delete Account.

2. Your game account

Guest account

When you start playing, Fame Rush creates a guest account for you. No email, phone number or password is required. The account consists of a random account identifier, the nickname you choose (or a generated one) and a sign-in session stored on your device.

Game data on our server

To run the game and keep your save, our server stores:

  • your nickname, avatar and equipped cosmetics;
  • game progress: Coins, XP, Fame, level, league, daily streak, challenges, achievements, unlocked cosmetics and season results;
  • a history of the rewards you earn and spend, used to keep the game economy fair and to stop duplicate or forged rewards;
  • friend invite codes and friend challenges you create or accept;
  • an installation record: a random identifier created by the app on first launch (stored only in hashed form; it is not a hardware or advertising identifier), the platform, the app version and when the installation was first and last seen. It keeps a guest account tied to one installation and helps us detect abuse.

Our server, sign-in and database are provided by Supabase. Like any online service, Supabase processes technical request information such as your IP address to deliver and secure the service; Fame Rush's own game tables do not store your IP address.

What other players can see

Leaderboards and friend challenges show your nickname, avatar and cosmetics, level, league and Fame. Please do not use your real name or other personal information as your nickname.

3. Sign in with Google (optional)

You can choose Protect with Google to link your guest save to a Google account, or Continue with Google to open an existing save on a new device. Google then shares your Google account identifier, email address, name and profile picture link with our sign-in provider (Supabase Auth), which stores them with your account. The sign-in screen may show our server address (a supabase.co domain).

We use this information only to link and restore your save. The app shows the linked email address to you in Settings. Your Google name is not used as your nickname and is not shown to other players. Fame Rush does not receive your Google password and does not request access to your Gmail, contacts, files or other Google data.

4. Advertising — CAS.AI and mediation partners

Fame Rush shows ads through Clever Ads Solutions (CAS.AI), which works with mediation partners. The game uses:

  • rewarded videos that you choose to watch for an in-game reward, such as an extra attempt or a temporary x2 Coin Boost;
  • interstitial ads shown between some games, not more often than every few minutes;
  • native ads displayed as a card in the activities list.

The advertising SDK starts when the app opens and loads ads in advance, so ad-related network requests can happen before you see or choose an ad. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), a consent form is shown before personalised advertising, and you can change your choices later in Settings → Privacy Options when that option is shown.

CAS.AI and the partner that serves an ad may process your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID), IP address, approximate location derived from it, device and operating-system details, language, app identifiers, ad requests, impressions, clicks and fraud-prevention signals. They use this to deliver, measure and, where you allow it, personalise ads. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID and opt out of ad personalisation in your Android settings.

Ads in Fame Rush are configured as not directed at children. Mediation partners vary by region and availability; current integrations include Google AdMob, AppLovin, Unity (ironSource), Liftoff Monetize (Vungle), Mintegral, Pangle, Meta Audience Network, InMobi, Yandex Ads, Amazon Publisher Services, Maticoo, YSO Network and CAS Exchange. See the CAS.AI Privacy Policy and the partner list in the in-app consent form for details.

5. Analytics and crash reports — Google Firebase

Firebase Analytics

We use Firebase Analytics to understand how the game is used: app opens, screens viewed, whether starting the game succeeded, and advertising events such as an ad being loaded, shown or rewarded, including the estimated ad revenue. Firebase also processes an app-instance identifier, device model, operating-system version, app version, language and approximate location derived from your IP address. We do not send your nickname, email address or account identifier to Firebase Analytics.

Firebase Crashlytics

Crashlytics receives crash reports and some non-fatal errors: stack traces, the time of the error, device and operating-system details, app version and an installation identifier. We do not attach your nickname, email address or account identifier to crash reports.

6. Information stored on your device

  • your sign-in session and the app's installation identifier;
  • a copy of your last loaded game state (kept for up to 7 days) so the game opens quickly;
  • short-lived records of game actions still being sent to the server, so a reward is not lost or granted twice;
  • sound, music, haptics and reduced-motion settings.

Android may include this data in your device backup according to your backup settings; such backups are managed by Google and you, not by us.

7. Permissions

Fame Rush uses internet and network-state access to reach the game server and load ads, vibration for haptic feedback, and the Advertising ID permission for ads. Some advertising SDKs declare further technical permissions (for example wake lock or Wi-Fi state). Fame Rush does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, photos or precise location, and does not send push notifications.

8. How we use information

  • to run the game, save your progress and restore it on another device;
  • to show leaderboards, seasons and friend challenges;
  • to show ads and grant rewards for rewarded videos;
  • to keep the game fair and secure, prevent cheating and abuse;
  • to fix crashes and improve the game.

Where the GDPR or similar laws apply, we rely on performance of our contract with you (running the game and your account), our legitimate interests (security, fraud prevention, fixing errors and improving the game) and your consent where it is required (personalised advertising).

9. Sharing and service providers

We share information only with the service providers that run Fame Rush for us: Supabase (server, sign-in and database), Google (Sign in with Google, Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics) and CAS.AI with its mediation partners (advertising). We may also disclose information when required by law or to protect rights, safety and security. These providers may process information in countries other than yours under their own safeguards for international transfers. More information:

  • Supabase Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy and Firebase privacy and security
  • CAS.AI Privacy Policy

10. Retention and deletion

We keep your account and game data while your account exists.

Delete in the app: open Settings → Delete Account and type DELETE to confirm. Your account is deleted immediately, together with your nickname, progress, reward history, installation records, friend invites and challenges you created, and the link to your Google account. Friend challenges created by other players stay with them, without your account. The app then also removes its local copy of your game.

Delete without the app: see Delete your Fame Rush account.

Deleted data can remain in our hosting provider's encrypted backups for a limited period until those backups are overwritten. Firebase keeps Crashlytics reports for 90 days and Analytics data according to our retention settings. Advertising providers keep their records under their own policies. Uninstalling the app removes the data stored on your device but does not delete your account on our server.

11. Children

Fame Rush is not directed at children under 13, and its ads are configured as not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, receive a copy of it, restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent and complain to your data-protection authority. You can delete your account in the app at any time. For other requests, write to strmsupport@gmail.com with your nickname and, if your account is linked to Google, the linked email address. We may ask for information to confirm that the account is yours.

13. Security

Connections to our server and providers are encrypted. Access to game data is limited to your own account, and the server checks every reward and purchase of in-game items. No system is completely secure, so keep your device and the app up to date.
