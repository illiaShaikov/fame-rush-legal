Fame Rush

A free arcade game for Android. Play quick activities, earn Coins, XP and Fame, collect cosmetics and climb the monthly season leaderboard.

  • Wheel, Reels, Prize Drop, Ice Fishing, Ban Hammer and Clicker.
  • Daily challenges, achievements, streaks and friend challenges.
  • A new season every month.

Coins, Fame and rewards are virtual items with no cash value. They cannot be bought, won or exchanged for real money or prizes.
